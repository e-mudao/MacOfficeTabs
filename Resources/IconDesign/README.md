Generated using the built-in image_gen tool on 2026-10-12.

Artwork: `MacOfficeTabs-icon-v1.png`. Integrated into the app bundle in version 0.2.9 (build 18). `scripts/build-icon.sh` generates the ten macOS icon representations and `scripts/build-app.sh` includes the resulting ICNS before signing.

## Design

A porcelain rounded-square base frames a white document with blue, green and orange tab shoulders. These colors evoke Word, Excel and PowerPoint, while the unified tab motif communicates document switching. No text or official Microsoft logos are used.

## Generation prompt

Output a single isolated icon, centered, square 1024x1024 composition, with genuine transparent pixels outside its rounded-square silhouette. No presentation sheet, no labels, no text, no letters, no watermark, no surrounding scenery. Visual direction: elegant Apple macOS dock icon with a softly rounded square warm porcelain-white base, subtle beveled edge and restrained dimensional depth. Main glyph: one clean unified document-window shape with THREE prominent tab shoulders along its top edge, blue at left, green at middle, warm orange at right, evoking the three Office document types. The blue selected tab merges into a large ivory document panel in front; green and orange tabs clearly visible behind the upper edge, arranged horizontally in a coherent tab bar, not three disconnected icons. One small folded paper corner and just two broad subdued slate-blue horizontal document lines inside the panel. Tabs should be chunky, geometric and easily identifiable at 32px. Main glyph occupies 72 percent of the base; icon base occupies about 90 percent of the image, with balanced transparent margins. Soft front-facing near-orthographic geometry, tactile satin enamel and paper, controlled subtle highlights, soft ambient occlusion. Clean high-end utility branding, quiet and precise. Strong silhouette and enough color contrast. Avoid Microsoft logos, W/E/P initials, tiny controls, thin details, browser address bars, stacked browser screenshots, exaggerated glass transparency, excessive shadows, sparkles, metallic chrome, text, extra objects.
